# NewSpawn (OreSpawn)

A fan-made port of the classic **OreSpawn** mod to **Minecraft 1.12.2** (Forge).

[![CurseForge](https://img.shields.io/badge/CurseForge-NewSpawn-f16436)](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/newspawn-oresspawn)

> ⚠️ NewSpawn is in **beta** and may contain bugs or conflicts with other mods.
> Found one? [Open an issue](../../issues/new/choose) so it can be fixed.

This repository is for **bug reports, crash reports and release notes**.
Downloads are on [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/newspawn-oresspawn/files) and in the [Releases](../../releases) of this page.

---

## What's inside

- **Creatures and bosses** from OreSpawn: T-Rex, Kraken, Mobzilla, The King, The Queen, giant dragons and many more. Fossil blocks hatch into creature eggs.
- **6 dimensions**: Utopia, Mining, VillageMania, Islands, Crystal and Chaos. Travel by right-clicking the right bug with an empty hand.
- **Armor, tools and weapons**, including the Big Bertha and the Royal Guardian gear.
- **Tameable pets**, including the Gamma Metroid, which mines for you and levels up to 100.
- **`/orespawn` menu (O key)**: an in-game encyclopedia for creatures, tameables, your pets, blocks, items, structures and dimensions, with 3D previews, recipes and per-world spawn settings. It also lists creatures, items, blocks and dimensions from your other installed mods.
- **Additional Structures**: 171 optional structures for the Overworld, the Nether and The End. They all start disabled, and bigger ones are rarer.
- **Boss and pet health bars**, Wings of Creation, Elemental Apple, Giant Sequoias, creature territories and more.
- Available in **English** and **Portuguese (Brazil)**: messages follow your game language.

## Mod compatibility

Built in and switched on only when the other mod is installed. Without them, NewSpawn plays the same.

- **ProjectE**: every NewSpawn item has an EMC value.
- **Avaritia**: new singularities, Infinity versions of NewSpawn's 3D weapons, the Infinite Royal Guardian Armor, and Extreme Crafting and Compressor recipes shown in the `/orespawn` item pages.
- **ProjectE + Avaritia**: Avaritia items also get EMC.

## Requirements

- Minecraft **1.12.2**
- Forge **14.23.5.2847** or newer
- Optional: ProjectE, Avaritia

## Reporting a bug

1. Check if it was already reported in [Issues](../../issues).
2. [Open a new issue](../../issues/new/choose) using the **Bug report** or **Crash report** form.
3. For crashes, attach the file from `crash-reports` and your `logs/latest.log`.
